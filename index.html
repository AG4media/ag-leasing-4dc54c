Security, data & hosting RFP Sections 18 and 6

Your data, in the Gulf, under your control.

Gulf

Hosted in Microsoft Azure's Gulf regions — Qatar Central (Doha) or UAE North (Dubai) — so DPS data stays in the region. Separate database and document store for DPS. Sub-processors disclosed.

15 min / 4 h

Recovery point and recovery time objectives. Continuous backup, 30-day retention, tested restores, documented DR plan.

MFA · SSO

Role-based access with segregation of duties, Microsoft 365 single sign-on, AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, full audit logs.

AI you control

Our own Lease AI and assistant, running on Microsoft Azure's AI language models. Your documents are not kept and never train any model. A person approves every result.

Secure by design: role-based access, MFA, encryption, full audit logs, tested backups and continuous automated monitoring, hosted on Microsoft Azure — itself certified to ISO 27001 and SOC 2. Independent security testing available on request. Qatar Personal Data Privacy Protection Law observed. DPS owns its data and can export it in standard formats at any time — and receives it in full at the end of the agreement.
