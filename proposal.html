01Overview

What you asked for, and what we propose.

In shortYou want one modern system to run your leasing business: spaces, tenants, contracts, rent, cheques, documents, tenant requests and management reports — connected to your accounting and building systems, with a tenant portal and some AI help. We propose exactly that: a platform of 12 standard modules configured for DPS, delivered in six months, starting with your existing Excel list, and supported for five years at a fixed price.

What your RFP asks for

  • A long-term platform for leasing and tenant management — not just a database of contracts.
  • Strong standard features with as little custom development as possible.
  • Automation, workflows, dashboards, mobile access, a tenant self-service portal, and integrations with your other systems.
  • AI features, only where they are secure, explainable and checked by a person.
  • Migration of your current leasing list (157 records) into the new system.
  • A licensing table, a five-year total cost, a filled-in compliance checklist, and a live demonstration.

What we propose

  • Twelve modules that cover every part of your RFP: spaces and floor plans, leasing sales (CRM), contracts, billing and arrears, cheques, tenant documents, tenant portal, work permits and requests, renewals and move-in/move-out, reports, AI, and the workflow engine that runs approvals and alerts.
  • Everything connected. A contract signed in one place automatically creates the invoices, cheque schedule, document reminders and dashboard figures everywhere else.
  • Your Excel list first. We load, check and reconcile your 157 records in the first five weeks, and show you the result.
  • Live in six months, with working software shown to you every week from week six, and a formal handover at the end.
  • One simple price — a yearly subscription with everything included, fixed for five years.

Properties, buildings and spaces

Leasing sales and the deal pipeline (CRM)

  • Approvals go to the right manager automatically.
  • Dashboards show how fast and how often enquiries become contracts (enquiry-to-offer, offer-to-contract, time-to-lease), and why deals are lost.

RFP Section 5

Lease contracts

You asked for
  • Create the lease from the approved offer; handle amendments, assignments, subleases and termination
  • Rent plus other charges (service charge, utilities, marketing, parking…)
  • Rent-free and fit-out periods; rent increases (fixed, percentage or index-linked)
  • Break and renewal options, notice periods, key dates
  • Versions, audit trail, contract documents, attestation, e-signature
What you get
  • A Lease Administration module. The lease is created from the approved deal with no re-typing; amendments, addenda, assignments, subleases and terminations are recorded against it.
  • Rent and every other charge (CAM — "common area maintenance", i.e. service charge — utilities, marketing fund, parking, storage) are separate lines, each with its own billing frequency.
  • Rent-free periods and yearly increases are entered once and the invoices follow automatically.
  • Key dates (renewal notice, expiry, break options) create reminders by themselves.
  • Contract documents are generated from DPS's own templates and carry all the information a written lease must contain under Qatar's property leasing law — the parties' details, the premises, the term, the rent and payment method, the permitted purpose and the agreed terms.
  • Every change creates a new version; nothing is lost. The attestation reference and attested copy are stored on the lease. Contracts are signed electronically through the e-signature provider DPS selects (provider fees excluded); internal sign-offs use the platform's own electronic approvals.

RFP Section 7

Rent, invoicing and arrears

You asked for

  • Create invoices here or in your accounting system
  • Record payments, receipts, outstanding amounts and ageing
  • Automatic reminders and escalation for late payers
  • Forecast future rent
  • Turnover rent (percentage of tenant sales) and reports on revenue leakage
What you get
  • Invoices are created automatically from the lease on whatever schedule you set — or sent to your accounting system to be created there.
  • Payments are matched to invoices automatically (from cheques, online payments or bank statements). You always see who owes what and for how long ("ageing").
  • Reminders go out by portal, email, WhatsApp or SMS after the number of days you choose, and escalate to a manager if unpaid.
  • Future rent is forecast from the contracts you already have.
  • Tenants can submit sales figures; turnover rent is calculated for you.
  • "Revenue leakage" reports find money you should be collecting but are not: missed rent increases, unbilled charges, expired leases still occupied.

Cheques and post-dated cheques (PDC)

You asked for

What you get

  • Finance is alerted before cheques fall due; tenants can be reminded too.

Design principles

One record of the truth. Every module reads and writes the same data, so a lease signed in one place updates invoices, cheques, documents and dashboards everywhere.
Configuration, not programming. Statuses, charges, request types, approval routes and alert rules are settings that DPS administrators can change.
Open by design. Every function the screens use is also available through the API, so future systems and properties can be connected.
Secure by default. Access by role, two-factor login, encryption, audit trail and backups are built into every layer (section 06).

11Glossary

Terms used in this document.

AMCAnnual maintenance contract — a yearly fee for updates and support when you buy software outright. APIA standard, documented way for two software systems to exchange data automatically. Arrears / ageingMoney tenants owe you, and how long it has been outstanding (for example 1–30 days, 31–60 days). AttestationOfficial certification of a lease contract by the relevant authority. CAFM / CMMSThe software a facilities or maintenance team uses to manage work orders in a building. CAM / service charge"Common area maintenance" — the charge tenants pay towards shared areas and services, on top of rent. CRCommercial Registration — a company's registration certificate issued by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. CRM"Customer relationship management" — the tool used to track prospects, offers and deals. DashboardA screen that shows the key figures of the business at a glance, updated automatically. EncryptionScrambling data so it can only be read by authorised systems and people. ERP"Enterprise resource planning" — a company's main accounting and finance system. E-signatureSigning a contract electronically instead of on paper. Fit-outThe works a tenant does to prepare a unit before opening; often rent-free. HypercareThe period of close, intensive support just after a new system goes live. IntegrationConnecting two systems so data flows between them without re-typing. Lease abstractionPulling the key terms (dates, rent, options…) out of a lease document into structured fields. MFA / two-factorLogging in with a password plus a code from your phone, for extra security. ModuleOne part of the platform dedicated to one area of work (for example "Cheques"). On-premiseSoftware installed on your own servers rather than in a data centre (not offered in this proposal). PDCPost-dated cheque — a cheque written today with a future date, commonly used for monthly rent. Power BIMicrosoft's reporting tool; your analysts can connect it to the platform's data. Private cloudA separate environment in a data centre, used only by you (not offered in this proposal). QIDQatar ID card. Rent rollThe complete list of your leases and the rent each one pays — your total rental income. RPO / RTORecovery targets after a failure: how much recent work could be lost at most (RPO) and how long until the system is back (RTO). SaaS"Software as a service" — using software over the internet for a subscription, hosted and run by the supplier. SLAService level agreement — the written promise of how fast we respond and how available the system is. SSOSingle sign-on — logging in with your existing company (Microsoft 365) account. TCOTotal cost of ownership — everything you pay over the whole period, not just the licence. Tenant portalA website tenants can install on their phone like an app, where they see their lease and charges, pay, upload documents and submit requests. Turnover rentRent calculated as a percentage of the tenant's sales, usually for retail. WALEWeighted average lease expiry — the average time remaining on your leases, weighted by rent; a measure of how secure your income is. WorkflowA sequence of steps and approvals the system follows automatically, for example for a work permit.
